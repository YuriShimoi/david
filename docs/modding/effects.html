Built-in effects

Every effect and adjustment, with the ids the CLI, Lua (doc:applyEffect) and the dialogs share. Colors are written #rrggbb or #rgb; a choice takes its name or index; a curve is x:y/x:y per channel, channels split by |.

david-cli in.png --effect gaussian-blur:radius=3 --effect outline:color=#000 -o out.png
